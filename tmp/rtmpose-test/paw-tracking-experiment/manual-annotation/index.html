Identity and event guidance

For dog02, record touchdown (first visible ground contact) or toe-off (first frame visibly clear of ground) only when supported by consecutive frames. Use uncertain if identity, contact, or timing is unclear; “no event” means explicitly reviewed. Events are independent human observations, never filled from model extrema.

Temporal identity adjudication

You may reveal model predictions for visual inspection even when human labels are uncertain or unmarked. Prefer completing independent human labels first when possible. Inspect the model explicitly to judge whether its pair’s identity mapping changes between frames. A foot crossing naturally is not a label swap. Both frames must have certain, visible human points for both paws before a definite verdict is allowed. Persistent reversed labels at both frames are a per-frame identity error, not necessarily a new swap at this transition.

Human markers: hollow circles. Model markers: crosses, shown only after Reveal. No audit flags or costs are shown here to reduce review bias. Revealing the model is recorded as exposure in the annotation export.
